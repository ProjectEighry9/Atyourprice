How it works

Three steps to moving day.

Not sure what's fair? Check the price on any three moving sites, then offer us a better one.

01

Name your price

Fill in the short form: what's moving, where, when, and the price you think is fair for the job.

02

Yes or no in 60 minutes

If we can do the job at your price, you get a yes. If not, you can change your offer and send it once more.

03

We move it

A manager calls to confirm the details, a 50% deposit holds your van and date, and we arrive and get you moved.

Good to know

  • The 50% deposit is only asked for after a yes and a call from our manager.
  • The other 50% is paid once loading is done, by bank transfer or cash to the driver.
  • A no always comes with a clear reason, so your second offer can land.
  • After two no's you can send a new request 24 hours later.
