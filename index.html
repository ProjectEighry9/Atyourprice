Our advice

Before you move, check five things.

  • Choose a registered company

    Make sure the company is listed at Companies House on GOV.UK, so a real business stands behind the job and you can always reach it.

  • Agree every detail first

    Before any prepayment, agree the date and time, both addresses, the full list of items, any dismantling, the van size or an extra trip, and the number of movers.

  • Check access and parking

    Narrow streets, limited parking or restricted access? Check the options in advance and tell us, so we can plan ahead.

  • Travelling with your things?

    Ask in advance whether we can take one or two passengers. It depends on how many movers are in the van.

  • Confirm the route

    Check whether your things go straight to the new address, or whether other collections happen on the way.
